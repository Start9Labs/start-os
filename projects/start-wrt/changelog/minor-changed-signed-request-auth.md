- **Sign-in uses a key your browser holds.** Logging in creates a key the browser keeps but cannot export, and every request is signed with it, so no reusable credential crosses the network. This update signs everyone out once. A sign-in now survives later updates and lasts until you log out or leave it unused for 30 days, instead of expiring after a day.
- **`startwrt-cli auth login` enrolls a key** at `~/.startwrt/id.key.pem` instead of saving a cookie, and `startwrt-cli auth session list` and `auth session kill` show and revoke sign-ins. On the router itself the CLI needs no login, and `auth login` and `auth logout` say so.
